ResultsCompared with healthy controls, BC patients had higher levels of thyrotropin (TSH), total triiodothyronine (TT3), total thyroxine (TT4) and thyroglobulin and lower levels of thyroid peroxidase antibody (TPOAb). Compared with controls, both NMIBC patients and MIBC patients had elevated TT3 and TT4 levels. The proportions of NMIBC and MIBC patients were significantly greater in the high TSH, TT3, and TT4 groups than in the low TSH, TT3 and TT4 groups.
