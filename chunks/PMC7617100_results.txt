ResultsCross-sectional analysis showed higher median IFN-α2 levels in the active/intermediate group (median 3185 fg/ml, IQR 48–13 703) compared with the LDAS (571 fg/ml, IQR 57–1310 fg/ml, P = 0.04) and remission sub-groups (271 fg/ml, IQR 3–56, P <0.001). IFN-α2 was higher in all JSLE patients (median 587 fg/ml, IQR 11–2774) as compared with JIA patients (median 7 fg/ml, IQR 3–236, P = 0.0017) and HCs (P = 0.017). JSLE patients in remission or LDAS with abnormal IFN-α2 levels had a shorter time to flare over the subsequent six months compared with those with normal IFN-α2 levels (P = 0.022). Longitudinally, multivariable analysis demonstrated high IFN-α2 to be the only predictor of an ongoing flare (P = 0.028).
