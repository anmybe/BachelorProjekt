Biomarker Group

Relevance for Sports
--------------------
9 / 10

Activity Context
----------------
Highly trained male distance runners undergoing a 4-week intensified altitude training camp (~1700–2200 m) with structured exercise challenges (weekly downhill running bouts, 3000m time trials) while supplemented with 1000 mg/day of Urolithin A or placebo.

Effect Summary
--------------
Skeletal muscle protein expression of MRPS11 significantly increased (p = 0.012 versus placebo) following 4 weeks of Urolithin A supplementation compared to placebo in highly trained male distance runners during an altitude training camp.

Sport Implications
------------------
An increase in MRPS11, a mitochondrial ribosome protein subunit involved in mitochondrial protein synthesis, suggests enhanced mitochondrial adaptation and biogenesis in skeletal muscle. This is a crucial physiological response for endurance athletes, contributing to improved aerobic capacity, metabolic efficiency, and potentially faster recovery from intense training. The observed upregulation of MRPS11 expression under Urolithin A supplementation indicates a mechanism through which mitochondrial pathways are enhanced, which may underpin reported improvements in VO2max and reductions in post-exercise muscle damage and perceived exertion.
