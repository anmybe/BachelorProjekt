Activity Context
----------------
The study involved collegiate athletes and focused on identifying novel diagnostic plasma biomarkers for sport-related concussion (SRC). Blood samples were collected from concussed and non-concussed athletes within 48 hours post-injury, analyzed in acute (0-6 hours) and subacute (7-48 hours) phases.

Effect Summary
--------------
Plasma levels of C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 6 were significantly lower after sport-related concussion (SRC) in collegiate athletes compared to controls.

Sport Implications
------------------
C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 6 (CXCL6) is identified as a potential candidate biomarker for the diagnosis of sport-related concussion (SRC). While not directly related to monitoring typical training load, adaptation, or performance, its utility as an injury diagnostic tool is highly relevant for athletes. Objective assessment of SRC can significantly inform injury management, recovery protocols, and safe return-to-play decisions, all of which directly impact an athlete's ability to resume training and optimize performance.
