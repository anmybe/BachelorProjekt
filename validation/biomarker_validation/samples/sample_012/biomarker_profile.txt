Relevance for Sports

Activity Context
----------------
This study involved both untrained and resistance-trained male participants undergoing 8 weeks of progressive high-intensity resistance training. The intervention included daily 5g Cistanche deserticola (CD) extract supplementation.

Effect Summary
--------------
In participants supplementing with Cistanche deserticola (CD) alongside resistance training, central nervous system Dopamine concentrations were observed to be higher and maintained at elevated levels. Specific quantification of the magnitude was not provided.

Sport Implications
------------------
Elevated and maintained Dopamine levels, as observed with Cistanche deserticola supplementation during resistance training, may contribute to enhanced athletic performance by improving neurotransmission and neuromuscular coordination. Furthermore, higher Dopamine is implicated in mitigating central fatigue and alleviating tension/anxiety, which can be beneficial for sustaining high-intensity training and facilitating recovery from mental exertion.
