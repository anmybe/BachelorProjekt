Biomarker

Isometric Muscle Strength

Biomarker Group
---------------
muscle_damage

Relevance for Sports
--------------------
9 / 10

Activity Context
----------------
The findings are based on a systematic review evaluating soy protein (SP) and soy peptide supplementation in active individuals and athletes. Specific context includes trained boxers supplementing with 42.2 g SP/day for 4-6 weeks, with measurements taken after Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage (EIMD) exercise. Supplementation protocols varied widely in dose (e.g., 0.5 g/kg BW/day to 90 g/day) and duration (acute to 12 weeks), often compared against whey protein or placebo.

Effect Summary
--------------
Soy protein supplementation was shown to improve isometric strength, specifically in trained boxers recovering from exercise-induced muscle damage, indicating enhanced muscle function and recovery.

Sport Implications
------------------
Improved isometric muscle strength, especially in the context of recovery from Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage (EIMD), signifies enhanced muscle repair and functional restoration. This directly impacts an athlete's ability to recover from strenuous training sessions, maintain high-intensity training loads, and return to peak performance levels more quickly. It suggests that soy protein supplementation could be an effective nutritional strategy to support post-exercise recovery and adaptation, contributing to consistent training and improved athletic performance by mitigating the functional deficits associated with muscle damage.
