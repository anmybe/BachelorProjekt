Biomarker

MRPL39

Biomarker Group

Relevance for Sports
--------------------

Activity Context
----------------
Highly trained male distance runners participated in a 4-week intensified altitude training camp (~1700–2200 m) with structured exercise challenges (weekly downhill running bouts and 3000 m time trials). During this period, they received daily supplementation of either 1000 mg Urolithin A (UA) or a placebo.

Effect Summary
--------------
MRPL39 protein expression in skeletal muscle biopsies showed statistically significant increases (p = 0.001 versus placebo) following 4 weeks of Urolithin A supplementation, independent of training alone. MRPL39 is identified as a mitochondrial ribosome protein subunit, involved in mitochondrial protein synthesis.

Sport Implications
------------------
The upregulation of MRPL39 in skeletal muscle, a component essential for mitochondrial protein synthesis, suggests an enhancement of mitochondrial biogenesis and function in highly trained endurance athletes when combined with Urolithin A supplementation during intensified altitude training. This indicates a stronger impact on mitochondrial pathways, which are critical for aerobic energy production, endurance performance, and adaptation to training stress. Monitoring MRPL39 could serve as a diagnostic indicator for assessing the effectiveness of interventions aimed at optimizing mitochondrial function, potentially reflecting improved recovery capacity and enhanced training adaptation leading to better performance outcomes.
